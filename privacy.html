1. Scope and contact

This notice explains how the provider operating under the Highlightext brand handles personal information in connection with highlightext.com, its keyword tools, email requests, payments, and custom public-profile research. For questions, provider details, or privacy requests, email hi@highlightext.com.

The website demonstration and the browser extension have different capabilities. Third-party websites, email providers, social platforms, GitHub, Stripe, and Coinbase also process information under their own privacy notices.

2. Information and sources

  • Website and demo: keywords typed into the homepage demo are processed in that page. The demo code does not upload your terms, set cookies, store them persistently, or include analytics or advertising trackers. Hosting and network providers may process IP addresses, browser/device details, requested URLs, referrers, and request times to serve and secure the website.
  • Requests and support: the request forms prepare an email draft in your email app. Information reaches our inbox when you choose to send it. We then receive your email address, message, keywords, requirements, attachments, and any other information you send.
  • Orders and payments: payment providers collect the payment and billing information you give them. We may receive customer contact details, order identifiers, amount, currency, payment status, and transaction references to verify payment and arrange access. We do not collect full card details through this static website. Public blockchain transactions may expose wallet addresses and transaction details.
  • Public-profile research: to fulfill an agreed project, we may collect public handles, profile URLs, public display names or descriptions, and relevance notes from public platforms and customer-provided sources. These can identify people even when publicly available. We do not need sensitive attributes, private messages, or information about children for this service.
  • Browser extension: the current local extension processes pasted text and enabled page text in your browser. Its code does not upload that text. Settings and a downloaded word list are cached in browser local storage; a popup draft uses browser session storage. It fetches the public word list from GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure receives the request. Page access is activated by your click.

3. How we use information

We use relevant information to respond to inquiries, review submissions, provide agreed research or custom lists, verify payments, arrange access, support customers, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal and accounting obligations. We do not use the homepage demo text for advertising or AI-model training.

Where a legal basis is required, customer communications and delivery may be necessary to take requested steps or perform a contract; recordkeeping may be required by law; security, support, and relevant public-source research may rely on legitimate interests only where lawful after considering affected people’s rights. We obtain consent where required. Public availability alone is not a lawful basis, and we will not rely on a customer contract as a legal basis for processing unrelated people’s data.

4. Disclosures, research delivery, and publication

Information may be processed by providers of hosting, email, payment processing, and operational support as needed for their roles. Payment providers may also act independently for their own compliance and fraud-prevention purposes. We may disclose information when legally required, to protect lawful rights or security, or in a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards.

We deliver agreed public-profile research, including relevant public handles, links, and notes, to the customer who commissioned it. Providing personal information in a paid research deliverable may qualify as a sale or other regulated disclosure under applicable privacy laws. We do not make a blanket claim that public-profile information is exempt. Contact us to object or request exclusion from research deliverables, or to exercise an applicable opt-out right. We must assess and satisfy applicable notice, opt-out, and data-broker obligations before undertaking covered activity.

Word suggestions accepted for the public word repository may be visible to anyone and copied by others. Do not submit private information for publication. We do not intentionally publish your email address, private research brief, or entire support correspondence as part of a word suggestion. This website has no advertising pixels or cross-site behavioral advertising scripts.

5. Cookies and privacy signals

The current static website does not set its own cookies, use browser storage for the demo, or deploy tracking analytics. External checkout, email, hosting, and linked platforms may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their policies. Following a link takes you to that provider.

The website does not perform cross-site behavioral tracking and does not change its operation in response to Do Not Track. Where legally required, we must honor applicable opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control for covered processing. A signal on this static site may not identify a separate public profile; you can also email us a profile URL to request exclusion without creating an account.

6. Retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, including providing the service, customer support, required tax/accounting records, fraud prevention, legal claims, and maintaining suppression records to honor objections. The appropriate period depends on the record and applicable legal requirements. You can request deletion; information subject to a legal retention requirement may be retained with use restricted to that purpose.

Local extension settings and cached lists remain until you clear extension storage or uninstall it; popup session data is temporary. Public repositories, third-party copies, and blockchain records may remain available outside our control. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Do not send passwords, seed phrases, financial account credentials, or sensitive personal records through our forms or email.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access or receive a copy of your information, know its sources and recipients, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, restrict processing, object to processing, obtain portability, withdraw consent, and opt out of sale, sharing, or certain profiling. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority and appeal a denied request where applicable. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising a right.

Email hi@highlightext.com with your request and, if relevant, the public profile URL or order email involved. You do not need to be a customer to raise a research-related privacy request. We may need proportionate verification or proof of an authorized agent’s authority; please do not send identity documents unless we request them through a suitable channel. We respond within applicable legal deadlines and explain any lawful refusal and available appeal route. To appeal, reply with “Privacy appeal” in the subject.

You may choose not to send a request or complete a purchase. To stop optional promotional emails, use an unsubscribe option if supplied or email us; essential order, support, and legal communications may still be sent. Clear extension storage to remove local settings and cached lists.

8. International processing

Hosting, email, payment services, customers, and public platforms may process information in countries other than yours, where privacy laws may differ. Where law requires a transfer safeguard, covered transfers must use a valid mechanism, such as applicable contractual safeguards or an adequacy decision. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your information.

9. Children and sensitive data

The Services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 18. Do not submit children’s information or request research targeting children. If you believe a child’s information or sensitive personal data has been supplied, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

10. Changes and provider notices

We will post updates with a revised date and provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes where required. New practices do not retroactively authorize incompatible uses of previously collected information.
